LibriaTV is an unofficial AniLiberty (formerly AniLibria) client for Android TV and TV boxes, built for a remote control. Content is in Russian.

<b>Features</b>
• Sign in with a phone code or login/password
• New episodes, Continue watching, airing today and tomorrow, recommendations
• New-episode notices for favorites and an Android TV home-screen channel
• Favorites and lists: Watching, Planned, Watched, On hold, Dropped
• Catalog with filters, search (including voice), weekly schedule
• Player 480/720/1080p: opening/ending skip, speed, sleep timer, night sound; progress syncs with your account
• Torrents via TorrServe with episode picking
• Phone remote, themes and accent colours, self-update

Not affiliated with the AniLiberty team or the AniLibria studio. All content belongs to its rights holders and is streamed from AniLiberty servers.
